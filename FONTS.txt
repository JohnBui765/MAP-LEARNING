Fonts used by Map Learning for China’s names
==============================================

The map labels and the table use Be Vietnam Pro (OFL.txt) for Vietnamese and English, and the two
Noto Sans SC files below for Chinese characters and pinyin. The facts panel shows each Chinese name in
the KaiTi (handwriting) style of AR PL UKai CN, the font Hanzi Grid prints its practice sheets with.
All three are MODIFIED (cut-down) copies, made by make_cn_fonts.py from the full fonts kept in the
Hanzi Grid repository (CHINESE-HANDWRITING-PRACTICE-1/fonts/source/). The same notice is written into
each file’s "Description" name record.

noto-sans-sc-400-subset.woff2
  Modified version of Noto Sans SC for Map Learning: the Regular (400) weight of the variable font, reduced on 2026-11-15 with fontTools to 127 Chinese characters (China’s provinces, capitals and abbreviations) plus Latin, pinyin and punctuation; hinting removed; saved as WOFF2. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL-notosanssc.txt).

noto-sans-sc-600-subset.woff2
  Modified version of Noto Sans SC for Map Learning: the SemiBold (600) weight of the variable font, reduced on 2026-11-15 with fontTools to 127 Chinese characters (China’s provinces, capitals and abbreviations) plus Latin, pinyin and punctuation; hinting removed; saved as WOFF2. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL-notosanssc.txt).

ukai-cn-subset.woff2
  Modified version of AR PL UKai CN for Map Learning: reduced on 2026-11-15 with fontTools to 127 Chinese characters (China’s provinces, capitals and abbreviations); hinting removed; saved as WOFF2. Licensed under the Arphic Public License (see LICENSE-ukai.txt).
